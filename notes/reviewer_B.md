## Numerical audit and independent checks

The saved small-grid corpus has 66 programs: 48 synthetic (37 sensitive), 12 canonical (8 sensitive), and 6 rule-derived (5 sensitive). There are 50 sensitive and 16 safe cases overall, with 19,940 oracle assignments and 330 method-result rows. Both exact methods complete on every case and match all saved exhaustive maxima. Automatic dispatch uses 22 residue-DP, 19 reduced-SMT, and 25 unreduced-SMT runs. Random testing misses `threshold_03` and `threshold_08`; Hypothesis misses `currency_conversion`. The independent-interval baseline warns on 15 of 16 safe cases.

I checked the scaling maxima with a separate exact Fraction nearest-even implementation, reading the declared rate denominators directly from the JSON line strings rather than importing the analyzer or its parser. Each line's local errors were enumerated over its independently derived period `2*d` or its shorter interval. Their summed extrema plus the half-cent outer error give an integer upper bound. I checked the saved witness bounds and replayed its aggregate and per-line values with that independent routine. All 24 scaling certificates attain the independent upper bound. This is a valid bound-plus-witness certificate; obtaining the witness from the tested DP does not make maximality circular because the bound and replay are independent.

The universal nearest-even bound is strictly loose in 10/24 scaling cases. At 64 lines it is 32 cents, whereas attainable maxima are 27 cents for `U=3` and 30 cents for `U>=20`. At 32 lines the corresponding maxima are 14 and 15 cents versus a universal bound of 16. Thus the benchmark does demonstrate the central distinction between an unrestricted bound and an attainable bound, including a gap that remains after complete local-period coverage.

The final snapshot has DP 72/72 exact completions, reduced SMT 36/72, and full SMT 26/72. Of unfinished reduced-SMT runs, 26 are UNKNOWN and 10 are SENSITIVE with an unestablished optimum; the full-SMT counts are 31 and 15. These counters and wall times are machine-dependent and must come from regenerated macros/tables. They must never be substituted for one another.

## Baselines, ablation, and threats to validity

The exact baseline and the residue-SMT ablation share the same symbolic encoder, rounding semantics, optimizer, and solver settings. This is an appropriate controlled way to isolate representative-box reduction. Comparing the reduced SMT path with the independent-line DP isolates the additional separable recurrence. It is not a comparison with independently developed verifier implementations. Structural reduction does not guarantee lower wall time: solver arithmetic, formulation overhead, and heuristic choices can dominate the reduced input bounds.

Random and Hypothesis methods answer a witness-search question; they do not prove equivalence or compute an exact maximum. Their misses should be described as missed witnesses rather than false safety proofs. `max_examples=64` configures generation and does not by itself establish a matched number of total predicates after shrinking, replay, or deduplication. The naive interval baseline deliberately loses shared-input correlation. Its high warning count cannot be generalized to Fluctuat, Daisy, or an advanced relational abstract interpreter.

Three sequential repetitions, one machine, fixed method order, no documented warm-up, and no randomized timing order limit quantitative runtime inference. Medians and raw repetitions are useful descriptive evidence, but there is no uncertainty estimate or cross-machine generalization. CPU model, RAM, and activity/load information would strengthen the environment record. A full denominator sweep is especially relevant because the algorithm explicitly declines `M>400`; the present suite cannot establish performance near that cutoff.

The six rule-derived fragments are transparent and do not copy external implementation code. Their source URLs and sections are metadata, not evidence that an industrial implementation was analyzed. This audit does not independently certify source-law transcription. The HMRC example is insensitive on the chosen grid: cent ticks at a 20% rate already yield multiples of 0.2 cent, so tenths-of-a-penny line rounding changes no raw line. Retaining this safe fragment is useful and supports the importance of grids. The 66-cent SSA discrepancy comes from an explicitly illustrative offset and ordering alternative, and cannot be reported as a known SSA defect.

## Implementation and reproducibility observations

The parser restricts execution through Python AST construction, rejects attribute access and nonlinear products/divisors, rejects float literals and numeric JSON float quanta, and explicitly resolves finite optional sites. The analysis incorporates tick quanta into affine coefficients. DP rejects repeated line dependencies and checks the exact raw-line minimum for sign-sensitive modes. The min/max predecessor maps reconstruct feasible assignments without copying full paths on every update. Solver maxima use integer-scaled objectives and replay before a complete result is returned. These are meaningful safeguards consistent with the paper contract.

The oracle does not import the Fraction quantizer or symbolic encoder, which provides useful arithmetic independence. It shares the AST, so it cannot detect parser mistranslation by itself. Known-output parser integration tests and rejection cases partially address this risk. The project tests check the six unit-quantum rounding modes, structural parity, signed-mode rejection, DP/exhaustive agreement, benchmark replay, CLI JSON, and quantum guards. The separate proof sanity program adds more varied coefficients, signs, quanta, and signed extrema. These are bounded validations, not proof-assistant verification.

The public JSON interface lacks general expression-size or input-bit limits beyond optional-site enumeration and the DP state cap. Very large expressions, rational constants, or policy families can consume substantial preprocessing memory/time before solver timeouts apply. This is a resource-management limitation for untrusted service deployment, not an arbitrary-code-execution finding. It should be documented if the CLI is exposed as a service; the current local research prototype does not warrant a security certification claim.

## Late substantive algorithm revision: parity compression

After further independent review, the original cyclic-residue DP was found to retain more terminal state than the common-mode batch objective requires. For normalized raw lines, write `k_i=R_i(y_i)`, `f_i=y_i-k_i`, `K=sum k_i`, and `F=sum f_i`. The final signed discrepancy is `R_0(K+F)-K`. FLOOR and CEILING depend only on `F`; HALF_EVEN depends on `F` and `K mod 2`. For a fixed parity its terminal map is monotone, so two attained residual endpoints suffice. In the certified nonnegative HALF_UP/DOWN/UP extension, the terminal maps are `floor(F+1/2)`, `floor(F)`, and `ceil(F)`, even when `F` itself is negative. Applying the signed quantizer directly to `F` would be a defect.

I independently checked this argument before implementation and found no counterexample under the established independent-line premises. The implementation now uses exact Fraction residuals and each line's direct sufficient period `den(a_i/2)`; constants do not enlarge that period and no common coefficient denominator is constructed. It retains at most two prefix parity states, or one state for modes whose terminal map does not need parity. Local extrema, XOR composition, separate min/max backpointers, and replay of *both* signed endpoints establish the same witness contract as the old cyclic algorithm. The fixed per-line representative cap is 400; large coefficient denominators with narrow feasible intervals can succeed, while a wide interval with more than 400 representatives declines. Arithmetic-operation complexity after affine normalization is `O(sum_i min(T_i,width_i)+n)` with `O(n)` predecessor records, excluding rational operand bit costs. The theoretical proof is independently handled by Reviewer A.

The old algorithm remains `cyclic_batch`, exposed as `--method cyclic`, and reports `cyclic-dp`. The improved default is `dp_batch`, reporting `parity-dp`. It reports `parity_states`, `active_states`, `local_periods`, `local_representatives`, `transitions`, and separate signed-endpoint witnesses; it intentionally has no shared-denominator/modulus field. Affine preprocessing uses each expression's actual dependencies rather than an `n`-coordinate zero vector for every line.

New tests directly enumerate Fraction values using a separate distance-based quantizer, without calling the analyzer's concrete quantizer. Two Hypothesis suites cover 100 signed covariant cases and 100 certified-nonnegative sign-sensitive cases, comparing *both signed endpoints* and their attaining inputs against direct Cartesian enumeration and the preserved cyclic baseline. Explicit regressions cover odd line counts 1/3/5/7, half ties after odd integer shifts, negative FLOOR operands, positive HALF_UP/UP lines with negative residuals, nonunit input/output quanta, independent constant offsets with coprime large denominators, prime narrow/wide representative caps, shared-input rejection, exact affine cancellation, extra-policy fallback, and the cyclic CLI. All semantic cases in the full 107-test run passed; one error-message regex mismatch in a new name-collision test was corrected and its targeted rerun passed. The lead author performs the final complete run and four-method experiment regeneration.

This review also identified an input-policy preservation issue: `load_rule` could overwrite an explicit policy named `per_line` or `total` when generating batch variants. The lead author repaired model-level generated-name collision rejection, including placement-name collisions. The DP contract checks that its final family consists of exactly `per_line` and `total`; an extra explicit variant falls back to exact SMT and is included in the family spread. The corresponding regression verifies a three-cent result from an extra `x` policy rather than silently analyzing only the two generated policies.

The earlier 216-run numerical snapshot above documents the original cyclic-default review stage. The final revised experiment has four method paths and newly generated timing/completion macros; those records supersede the original runtime counts. The original discrepancy/ground-truth checks remain relevant, while final performance claims must use the revised saved results.
