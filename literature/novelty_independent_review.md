# Independent novelty and logic gate: RoundGuard

Reviewer: independent delegated agent. Audit date and search cutoff: 2026-10-03. This is a design-stage review, not a claim that an implemented artifact or finished manuscript has passed review. Scope: rational affine expression trees with explicit quantization, exact comparison of rounding policies, and the proposed separable invoice dynamic program. Primary publisher pages, author-hosted papers, arXiv full text, and the isl manual were checked. Targeted searches cannot establish that every relevant paper has been found.

## Gate decision

**The broad originality claim fails. The narrowed invoice-analysis contribution is conditionally plausible, but not yet established.** Shift periodicity of nested rounding trees is existing mathematics. Exact rational rounding in legal computations, symbolic detection of discrepancies, and backend rounding bugs are also prior work. The defensible target is a carefully delimited exact analysis: attainable extrema and concrete witnesses for heterogeneous bounded line-item grids, using structural periods and a finite cyclic-state dynamic program. The generic period fact, Presburger decidability, max/min-plus convolution, and witness backtracking must be attributed as foundations rather than presented as new discoveries.

A sound theorem plus working prototype would justify a reproducible research report. It would not by itself justify describing this as a strong theoretical novelty or an accepted publication. Publication strength depends on a nontrivial specialized result, credible realistic workloads, and comparisons with an exact Presburger/SMT encoding.

## Closest theoretical prior art

**Williams, The problem with integer programming (journal 2011; working paper 2010).** The author-hosted working paper has a tree representation of Chvátal functions, examples where nested ceiling operations can or cannot be removed, and an explicit shift-periodicity statement and example in PDF pages 21–23: a nested rational ceiling function satisfies f(x + 9) = f(x) + 6 and has average gradient 2/3. This directly undercuts a claim that rational rounding trees admitting affine drift plus periodic residuals are newly discovered. It does not, in the inspected passage, give RoundGuard's proposed mixed-mode invoice algorithm. [Primary working paper](https://personal.lse.ac.uk/williahp/pubs/WP%2010-118.pdf), [verified journal issue](https://academic.oup.com/imaman/issue/22/3?browseBy=volume), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/imaman/dpq014).

The correct DOI has `imaman`, not the `inaman` typo on an older author bibliography. Journal issue year is 2011; online publication and the working paper are 2010.

**Basu, Martin, Ryan, and Wang (2019).** Definition 2.2 explicitly constructs affine Chvátal functions from finite binary trees of rational affine leaves, positive rational scaling, addition, and ceiling. Section 3 removes rounding nodes using auxiliary integer variables and proves mixed-integer representability results. The syntax overlaps strongly with the positive-ceiling fragment. Signed scaling, explicit monetary quantum, and several tie conventions enlarge the implementation target, but this alone is not a compelling new mathematical principle. [Publisher](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/moor.2018.0967), [primary full text](https://arxiv.org/html/1711.07028v1).

**isl and Presburger arithmetic.** The isl manual, Section 1.4.13, supports quasi-affine expressions containing constants, integer variables, addition/subtraction, and integer division by a constant; it also supplies exact integer-set operations and lexicographic optimization. Rational fixed-quantum affine rounding can be encoded with integer auxiliary variables after clearing denominators. Consequently, exact equivalence and attainable maximum discrepancy for this fragment are already expressible in general-purpose exact frameworks. The proposed contribution must concern a useful reduction, specialization, or practical behavior rather than the existence of an exact decision procedure. [isl paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-15582-6_49), [official manual](https://libisl.sourceforge.io/manual.pdf), [Woods (2015)](https://doi.org/10.1017/jsl.2015.4).

Rhodes and Williams (1995) is an older source cited by Williams for Chvátal simplification and discrete subadditive functions. Its metadata and publisher extract were verified; its full proof was not available in this audit, so no specific theorem from it is claimed. [Publisher](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/mathematical-proceedings-of-the-cambridge-philosophical-society/article/abs/discrete-subadditive-functions-as-gomory-functions/6B534FE2D3CEB5817D0C67DD9078E114).

## Closest application and verification prior art

**Date arithmetic, ESOP 2024.** Sections 3–4 define paired semantics and a `sync` predicate for equality under opposite date rounding modes; an abstract interpretation proves rounding-insensitivity and supplies useful counterexample hints. This establishes the application pattern “formal rounding semantics plus automatic policy-insensitivity analysis for computational law.” It handles calendar ambiguity, not rational monetary residues. A monetary specialization can differ materially, but “first rounding-policy analysis for law” is untenable. [Publisher](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-57267-8_16), [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.08935v1).

**CUTECat, ESOP 2025.** Section 3, “Divisions and Rounding,” represents decimals as rationals and encodes nearest, ties-away-from-zero rounding in Z3 using a sign branch and floor. The authors report inconsistent rounding between Catala's Python backend and the reference interpreter and an upstream fix. Section 4 studies monetary witness readability. It covers richer language features than RoundGuard's affine fragment. It does not provide the proposed residue compression or exact global invoice extrema; that restricted distinction is defensible. [Publisher](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-91121-7_2), [author PDF](https://rmonat.fr/data/pubs/2025/2025-05-03_esop_cutecat.pdf).

**Daisy, FPTaylor, Fluctuat, fixed-point synthesis.** These already give sound numerical discrepancy/error analyses, propagation, optimization, and in some cases checked certificates. Daisy also covers fixed-point computations. Their usual guarantees are bounds against real semantics, generally allowing overapproximation, rather than an exact attainable discrepancy between prescribed monetary rounding policies. RoundGuard may exploit a narrower discrete problem to return exact extrema and witnesses. It must not imply that worst-case roundoff analysis, fixed-point verification, or numerical certificates are new. [Daisy](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-89960-2_15), [FPTaylor](https://soarlab.org/publications/2015_fm_sjrg/), [Fluctuat](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-18275-4_17), [fixed-point synthesis](https://lara.epfl.ch/~kuncak/papers/DarulovaETAL13SynthesisFixedPointPrograms.pdf).

**2026 check.** Monat and Huttner's February 2026 recommendations discuss arbitrary-precision monetary representation and testing in Recommendations 9–10; they do not give the proposed exact residue algorithm. Tao et al.'s May 2026 preprint studies probabilistic floating-point error using concentration inequalities and Taylor models; that is a different guarantee. Neither rescues the broad first-tool claim. [Monat and Huttner](https://doi.org/10.1080/13600834.2026.2628389), [author PDF](https://rmonat.fr/data/pubs/2026/2026-02-16_legal_code_recos.pdf), [Tao et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04232).

## Blocking semantic restrictions

1. **Translation covariance is a premise, not a property of all common rounding modes.** Floor, ceiling, and nearest with ties toward positive infinity commute with integer translation. Nearest ties-to-even commutes with even integer translation. Half-away-from-zero does not commute with any nonzero global translation over signed arguments: R(-1/2) = -1, but R(-1/2 + 2) = R(3/2) = 2, whereas R(-1/2) + 2 = 1. Truncation toward zero also fails across zero. The theorem must exclude these modes globally or prove sign preservation for every relevant intermediate argument and representative. Nonnegative inputs do not suffice when affine intermediates can be negative. Catala uses half-away, so calling mathematical half-up Catala-compatible would be wrong.
2. **The grammar must exclude variable multiplication/division, overflow, saturation, and unhandled branching.** Constant rational scaling is safe. Input-dependent quantum, a variable tax rate multiplied by amount, max/min, and piecewise tax bands need a separate treatment. Exact rational semantics are different from IEEE decimal floating point with a finite precision context.
3. **Equal erased slopes are sufficient to cancel drift, not a novelty theorem.** For globally covariant quantizers, compute coordinate shifts making every quantized child drift an admissible multiple of its quantum. Under structural induction, each expression has affine drift along those shifts. If two expressions have the same erased linear coefficients, their difference is periodic. Computed shifts are valid periods; minimality is not established and semantic cancellation can make them much larger than necessary.
4. **Domain feasibility must survive compression.** On a box, retain one feasible member of each represented residue class, for example lower_bound + ((residue - lower_bound) mod period). Blindly replacing a value with its least nonnegative residue can violate bounds. Coupled constraints require a residue-feasibility procedure; a Cartesian product of individually reachable residues can manufacture impossible witnesses.
5. **Magnitude independence has qualifications.** Enumeration cost is bounded by a product of periods, but periods can be exponential in coefficient bit length. Exact arithmetic still costs more on large operands; endpoint processing also depends on endpoint bit length. Say “independent of the number of grid points after period construction,” not unconditional constant-time independence of monetary range.

## Invoice dynamic program: soundness and originality assessment

Assume independent line domains, an integral scale B with z_i = B y_i integer, fixed quantization, and a rounding convention whose residual is invariant under translating z_i by 2B. Define line error d_i = B R_i(y_i) - z_i. For each local residue r in Z/(2B), retain the attainable minimum and maximum d_i, together with witnesses. A state at prefix k records s = sum(z_i) mod 2B and the minimum/maximum D = sum(d_i). Combining a line is cyclic min/max-plus convolution.

Let h(s) = B R_outer(s/B) - s. Then the exact total-rounding-minus-line-rounding discrepancy is (h(s) - D)/B. For every reachable s, its maximum uses the minimum attainable D and its minimum uses the maximum attainable D. Taking extrema over reachable s and following predecessor witnesses is exact. The algebra is valid because the outer residual is periodic and line domains are independent. If multiple d_i occur at a local residue, retaining both extrema is sufficient for extrema, though not for enumerating all attainable discrepancies.

This gives O(n (2B)^2) naive convolution work and O(2B) working storage, apart from local table construction and witness storage. Straight predecessor reconstruction uses O(n 2B) storage. These are arithmetic-operation bounds, and the procedure is pseudo-polynomial in B. With half-away or truncation, one must preserve sign regions; the formula is not automatically valid on signed inputs. The independent convolution also fails for a fixed invoice grand total, a shared discount variable, or another cross-line constraint without additional state.

**Novelty caution:** the recurrence is standard cyclic min/max-plus dynamic programming. Knapsack convolution literature, including the 2023 near-convex results and 2026 deterministic monotone results, must be acknowledged; those faster algorithms do not automatically apply because residue error tables can be nonmonotone. Sadakane et al. (2001) already study compact state graphs and dynamic optimization of low-discrepancy roundings, although they choose rounding outputs for a fixed sequence rather than optimize inputs under fixed monetary policies. Skala (2017), an explicitly non-peer-reviewed draft, studies consistency and minimax rounding of financial totals in forests. It addresses a different optimization, but is important adjacent financial-total prior art. [Sadakane et al.](https://doi.org/10.1007/3-540-48224-5_14), [Skala's primary draft](https://ansuz.sooke.bc.ca/professional/rounding.pdf), [Bringmann and Cassis](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.ESA.2023.24), [Jin et al. (2026)](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.ICALP.2026.119).

No exact duplicate of this heterogeneous bounded-grid invoice formulation was located in the targeted search. This is evidence of a possible application-specific gap, not evidence that min/max-plus convolution is new or a guarantee of originality.

## Strongest reject argument

“The manuscript combines three standard ingredients: shift-periodic quasi-affine/Chvátal expressions, exact integer/rational SMT encodings, and dynamic programming on a finite cyclic group. Equal-drift cancellation follows immediately, and witness backtracking is routine. Existing legal-software work already formalizes rounding-policy sensitivity and discovers monetary backend discrepancies. The invoice experiment may be an engineering illustration rather than a scientific contribution, particularly if the inputs are unconstrained independent boxes and the comparison baseline is naive Cartesian enumeration. The claimed amount-range independence hides denominator complexity and excludes common sign-sensitive modes.”

That objection is strong. It should shape the paper before implementation claims are frozen.

## What would change the conclusion

- A precise specialized theorem for **attainable** extrema under realistic heterogeneous rates, finite input grids, and tie conventions, with explicit handling of every sign/domain premise.
- A substantially better bound or algorithm exploiting structure of monetary residue tables beyond generic O(n B^2) convolution, or a useful extension to a nontrivial coupled domain.
- Reproducible exact comparisons with a carefully implemented QF_LIA/Presburger model, including equivalence cases, known-inconsistent cases, partial residue domains, non-power-of-ten denominators, and growing line counts. Solver timeouts must be separated from incorrect answers.
- Realistic source-derived workloads or published invoice rules, with all simplifications stated, and independent confirmation that the witnesses replay in the intended arithmetic backend.
- An honest contribution statement: a specialized exact analyzer built on established periodic and dynamic-programming ideas, with the novelty concentrated in its restricted reduction and verified behavior.

At this stage, **do not claim “first exact monetary rounding verifier,” a newly discovered periodicity theorem, a polynomial-time algorithm in total input bit size, or a fully audited legal tax calculator.** The project can continue under the narrower contribution, but the strongest originality requirement has not yet passed.
